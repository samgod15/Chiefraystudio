HOW TO ADD A ROLL (one shoot with its own page and story)

1. Make a folder here for the shoot. Start its name with a number so the rolls
   stay in order, e.g.  03-brunch-at-the-corner-cafe
   (On GitHub: Add file > Create new file, and type  03-brunch-at-the-corner-cafe/story.txt
   as the name. The "/" creates the folder.)
2. Put a file called story.txt in that folder, like the example below.
3. Upload the shoot's photos into the same folder (exported the usual way).

The newest roll (highest number) shows first. The roll's photos also show up in the
category you name, so you only upload them once.

---------- copy from here into story.txt ----------
title: Brunch at the Corner Cafe
place: Port Coquitlam
date: November 2026
client: Corner Cafe
category: business
cover: 02-eggs-benny.jpg

A few lines in your own words: who it was for, what they needed, how the day went,
what you were going for.

Leave an empty line between paragraphs.
---------- to here ----------

Every line at the top is optional except title. "category" is one of your categories
(street, people, landscapes, business, b&w, film). "cover" is the file name of the
photo to use on the roll's card; without it the first photo is used.
