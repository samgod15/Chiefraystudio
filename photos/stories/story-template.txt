HOW TO ADD A STORY (one shoot, its photos and the story behind it)

1. Make a folder here for the shoot. Start its name with a number so stories stay
   in order, e.g.  01-kings-barber
   (On GitHub: Add file > Create new file, and type  01-kings-barber/story.txt
   as the name. The "/" creates the folder.)
2. Fill in story.txt like the example below and commit it.
3. Open the new folder and upload the shoot's photos into it.

With "category: business" the shoot shows up as a card in /business. Visitors tap
the card ("Read the story") and see your story with the photos underneath. Anyone
who opens one of these photos elsewhere on the site also gets a "behind the shot"
link to the story. The newest story (highest number) shows first.

---------- copy from here into story.txt ----------
title: Fresh cuts at Kings Barber
client: Kings Barber
place: Port Coquitlam
date: November 2026
category: business
cover: 03-fade-closeup.jpg

A few lines in your own words: who it was for, what they needed, how the day went,
what you were going for.

Leave an empty line between paragraphs.
---------- to here ----------

Only "title" is needed. "category" is one of your categories (street, people,
landscapes, business, b&w, film). "cover" is the photo used on the card; without it
the first photo is used.
