HOW TO ADD A STORY (one shoot, its photos and the story behind it)

1. Make a folder here for the shoot. Start its name with a number so stories stay
   in order, e.g.  01-kings-barber
   (On GitHub: Add file > Create new file, and type  01-kings-barber/story.txt
   as the name. The "/" creates the folder.)
2. Fill in story.txt like the example below and commit it.
3. Open the new folder and upload the shoot's photos into it.

With "category: business" the shoot shows up as a card in /business (the newest
story gets the big card at the top). Visitors tap it and get the story page: your
cover photo big, the story, the client's words, then the photos, then a "Book a
shoot like this" button that opens the contact form with the shoot already named.
Anyone who opens one of these photos elsewhere on the site also gets a
"behind the shot" link to the story.

---------- copy from here into story.txt ----------
title: Fresh cuts at Kings Barber
client: Kings Barber
link: @[their instagram]
place: Port Coquitlam
date: November 2026
category: business
cover: 03-fade-closeup.jpg
delivered: 24 edited photos, 3 reels
quote: [what they said about the photos, in their words]
by: [their name], owner

A few lines in your own words: who it was for, what they needed, how the day went,
what you were going for.

Leave an empty line between paragraphs.
---------- to here ----------

Only "title" is needed; leave out any line you don't have.
- category: one of your categories (street, people, landscapes, business, b&w, film).
- cover: the photo shown big on the card and at the top of the story. Without it,
  the first photo is used.
- link: the client's Instagram (@handle) or website (https://...). Their name
  becomes a link to it.
- delivered: what you handed over, in a few words.
- quote / by: something the client said about the shoot, and who said it. Only use
  their real words, with their OK.
