client: Patina Brewing Co.
place: Port Coquitlam
category: business

I cook at Patina. When Gavin's feature went on the menu, I shot it to learn what real work feels like. It was one of my first shoots with my own camera, and my chef helped me pull it off. One hour, on a Saturday night, everything in a rush.

I got the white balance wrong and it was stressful, so I kept shooting and took as many pictures as I could.

I know it's not a good photo. I'm keeping it here anyway, because it's where I started. Every shoot after this one has to beat it.
